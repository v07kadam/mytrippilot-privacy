Privacy Policy for MyTripPilot

Effective Date: August 24, 2026

1. Introduction

MyTripPilot ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") is a travel planning and expense management application that helps users organize trips, track and split expenses, manage packing checklists, maintain trip information, and export trip-related information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information MyTripPilot collects, how it is used, how it is stored, and the choices available to users.

By using MyTripPilot, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, MyTripPilot may collect and process the following information.

2.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Password or authentication credentials required to authenticate your account
  • Account and session information required to maintain your signed-in session

Passwords are handled by the application's authentication service and are not stored by the App in plain text.

2.2 Trip Information

When you create or participate in a trip, the App may collect:

  • Trip name
  • Trip type
  • Destination or other trip-related information entered by you
  • Trip start and end dates, where provided
  • Trip budget, where provided
  • Names of trip participants
  • Trip invitation codes
  • Information associated with your participation in a trip

2.3 Expense Information

When you use the expense tracking functionality, the App may collect:

  • Expense amount
  • Currency
  • Expense category
  • Expense date
  • Expense notes or descriptions
  • Name of the person who paid
  • Names of expense participants
  • Expense split information
  • Settlement information
  • Budget and spending information derived from expenses

Expense information may contain financial information that you choose to enter into the App. MyTripPilot does not process bank account numbers, credit card numbers, or payment card details as part of its normal expense-tracking functionality.

2.4 Checklist Information

The App may store:

  • Packing checklist items
  • Checklist completion status
  • Custom checklist items
  • Trip-specific checklist information

2.5 Exported Information

When you use the export or sharing features, the App may generate:

  • CSV files containing trip and expense information
  • PDF-ready reports containing trip and expense information
  • Text summaries containing trip and expense information

Exports are initiated by the user. When you choose to share exported information using another application, that information may be processed according to the privacy policy of the application or service you choose to share it with.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information collected by MyTripPilot to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Authenticate users
  • Create and manage trips
  • Allow users to invite and collaborate with other trip participants
  • Store and synchronize trip information
  • Track and calculate travel expenses
  • Calculate expense splits and balances
  • Record settlements
  • Calculate budgets and spending summaries
  • Manage packing checklists
  • Generate reports and exports
  • Provide the features and functionality of the App
  • Maintain and improve the security, reliability, and performance of the App

We do not use your trip or expense information for purposes unrelated to providing and improving the App unless otherwise disclosed in this Privacy Policy.

4. Cloud Storage and Synchronization

MyTripPilot provides cloud synchronization so that trip and expense information can be stored and accessed across supported devices.

Information entered into the App may therefore be transmitted to and stored by our cloud infrastructure and service providers used to operate the App.

The cloud services used by MyTripPilot may process information such as:

  • Account identifiers
  • Email address
  • Trip information
  • Participant names
  • Expense information
  • Checklist information
  • Settlement information

The specific cloud infrastructure and third-party service providers used by the App may change as the App evolves. We will update this Privacy Policy when material changes to our data processing practices occur.

5. Local Device Storage

MyTripPilot may store certain information locally on your device to support application functionality and session persistence.

On supported native devices, authentication session information may be stored using the device's secure storage facilities.

On the web version, session information may be stored using browser local storage or equivalent browser storage mechanisms.

Local data may remain on your device until it is removed by the App, the user, or the device's operating system.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared with service providers that are necessary to operate MyTripPilot, such as:

  • Authentication providers
  • Cloud hosting and database providers
  • Infrastructure and security service providers

These providers process information only as necessary to provide their services to the App.

When you explicitly use the App's sharing functionality, such as sharing an expense summary through WhatsApp, email, or another application, the information you choose to share is transferred to the selected application. That application's own privacy policy will govern its handling of the shared information.

Trip information may also be visible to other members of a trip when you intentionally join or invite them to that trip.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed by MyTripPilot.

These measures may include:

  • Encrypted communication between the App and cloud services
  • Secure authentication mechanisms
  • Secure storage of authentication session information on supported devices
  • Access controls for cloud-stored information
  • Appropriate protection of application infrastructure

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

We retain account and trip information for as long as necessary to provide the App's functionality and maintain the user's account, unless the user requests deletion or a longer retention period is required by law.

Trip and expense information may also be retained as part of a shared trip until the relevant trip or associated data is deleted in accordance with the App's functionality and applicable data deletion procedures.

9. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their MyTripPilot account and associated personal data.

Account deletion should be available:

  1. From within the MyTripPilot application; and
  2. Through the external account-deletion mechanism provided at:
    Account Deletion Request

When an account deletion request is completed, we will delete the personal information associated with the account, subject to any information that we are legally required or legitimately permitted to retain.

Deletion of an account may also remove trips, expenses, checklists, settlements, and other data associated exclusively with that account, subject to the application's shared-trip data model.

10. Trip and Shared Data

MyTripPilot allows users to invite other people to participate in trips.

Information that you add to a shared trip, including participant names, expenses, balances, settlements, and checklist information, may be visible to other authorized members of that trip.

Users should avoid entering highly sensitive personal information into trip names, notes, expense descriptions, or other free-text fields.

11. Permissions

Depending on the version and features enabled in MyTripPilot, the App may request device permissions required to provide specific functionality.

The App will request permissions only when required for a particular feature.

If future versions introduce features such as receipt scanning, camera access, location services, notifications, or other device capabilities, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe how those permissions and associated data are used.

12. Third-Party Services

MyTripPilot may rely on third-party services to provide authentication, cloud infrastructure, storage, analytics, security, or other application functionality.

Third-party services may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

The App does not sell personal information to third parties.

13. Children's Privacy

MyTripPilot is not specifically directed toward children under the age required by applicable law.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App without appropriate authorization, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, our data practices, or applicable legal requirements.

When we make material changes, we will update the effective date shown at the beginning of this Privacy Policy and, where appropriate, provide additional notice within the App.

We encourage users to periodically review this Privacy Policy.
